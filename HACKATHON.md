# OpenAI WebMCP Challenge facts

Official pages: [Devpost challenge page](https://webmcp.devpost.com/) and [OpenAI challenge page](https://openai.com/webmcp-challenge/).

## Deadline

September 3, 2026 at 1:00 p.m. Pacific Time (22:00 in Europe/Rome).

## Top 10 prize package

Each of the top 10 submissions receives:

- $3,000 cash
- One year of ChatGPT Pro
- One Codex Micro keyboard
- OpenAI swag
- Additional prizes and credits from challenge supporters

## Required submission material

- A working live URL
- A public YouTube demo shorter than three minutes, with audio
- A public open-source repository with all needed source code and instructions
- An open-source license that is visible and detected at the top of the repository page
- Text explaining why the use case fits WebMCP, how it improves the experience, what people and agents can do together, and how WebMCP was implemented

Test the deployed app in ChatGPT's in-app browser.

## Judging criteria

- WebMCP Leverage
- Execution
- Potential Impact
- Creativity & Ambition
